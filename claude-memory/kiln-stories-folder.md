On 4 Nov 2026 the owner proposed a repo folder called 'Kiln Stories', holding stories of overfiring that did not work well and how they were troubleshot, for Gentoo users who may find them useful. Created on 5 Nov 2026, with the Rust story as the first.

**Why:** failures with numbers are rare and useful, and Kiln's measuring gives it real ones.

**How to apply:** the owner agreed the name `kiln-stories/` and the points below on 4 Nov 2026. One file per story, for people to read, separate from `lessons/`, which stays short notes for Claude. Each story gives real numbers from `results/`, wrong turns and Claude's own mistakes included. Say it was written with Claude. Each story ends with one bold philosophical sentence: the owner asked on 5 Nov 2026 for Claude to be bold with it and not worry how Reddit would react. Stories stay in Kiln's own repo: Gentoo's Council bans AI-made contributions, so they do not go to Gentoo as text or patches. The repo is public, so check each story for private details. First story: why our Rust compiler was slower than `rust-bin`, once that work is finished. See [[kiln-maximum-optimisation]] and [[reddit-post-ideas]].
